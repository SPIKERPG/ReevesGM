00 // HOW TO RUN THIS
"The facility is an archaeological descent — not a lore museum."
■ CAMPAIGN 1 DESIGN PRINCIPLE
■ FOUNDATIONAL RULE

The players are uncovering fragments of something buried, not being briefed on a setting. Do not explain what they find. Let terminology precede meaning. Let evidence precede significance. Let atmosphere precede answers. Every discovery should raise a larger question than it resolves.

WHAT PLAYERS LEAVE WITH

Nothing in RE-01 should confirm the central mystery. Players should leave Campaign 1 with dread, partial understanding, and a strong reason to go to Italy. They should not leave understanding what the Reset is, how it works, or who caused it.

THE TWO KNOWN CONFLICTS

PS-08 is not Mitchell. The body in PS-08 is a failed earlier candidate or a researcher. Mitchell is alive at Point Nemo. If players assume PS-08 is the Prime and conclude the system is defunct, navigate gently — the evidence is intentionally inconclusive.

The comms jam blocks UGD relay frequencies specifically. If asked why the UGD never noticed: the facility has been too low-priority to actively scan, and the jam presents as equipment silence rather than active interference.

■ THE HARDEST THING TO EXECUTE

The instruction to "not explain what they find" is the most important and the hardest. When players find terminology they don't understand — PS-series, semantic continuity, Hardware, recalibration — resist the pull to clarify. The strangeness is the information. Exposition is a last resort. Atmosphere is the primary tool.

01 // THE BRIEFING
SETTING // UGD DIV IV — COPENHAGEN, DENMARK // YEAR 5

The UGD's administrative headquarters occupies the former Ørestad district — rebuilt from the ground up rather than reclaimed from ruin. Orderly, functional, modern in the way that a diagram is modern. The party arrives separately and meets in a fourth-floor conference room.

Outside: floor-to-ceiling windows. The city looks like a proof of concept for civilization. Everything works. Nobody looks happy. Nobody looks unhappy. People walk between buildings as though completing tasks.

■ WHAT THE PARTY IS TOLD

"Research Establishment 01 was a Division IV-funded behavioral sciences facility in the former state of Indiana, United States. It was documented as pursuing aerosol therapy trials for SPIKE management and neurological treatment research. We have floor records for the upper two levels. The facility went dark at Year 0 and has not been reoccupied. Exterior scans indicate residual automated systems are still drawing on emergency power. Your objective is recovery and assessment: surviving documentation, structural status, outstanding risk factors. Return with whatever the facility contains."

MATERIALS PROVIDED
  • Floor plans for Floors 1 and 2 — clinical, UGD-standard. Floor 2 shows a freight elevator marked MAINTENANCE ACCESS — LOWER LEVEL with no annotation beneath it. The plan ends there.
  • Research mandate summary — approved gene therapy research, aerosol treatment trials, EEG studies. Language is careful and institutional. Nothing overtly alarming.
  • Dossier on Dr. Elias Reeves — one page. Public-facing biography. No photograph. Notes his initial WHO proposal was "deprioritized" in favor of "a more targeted therapeutic approach." His pivot to gene therapy described as a natural continuation. No publications, no collaborators, no outcomes cited.
  • SPIKE wands, standard issue — calibrated at 0.
■ THINGS FOR ATTENTIVE PLAYERS TO NOTICE

The Reeves dossier is one page for a man who ran a two-year UGD-funded facility. The freight elevator on Floor 2 has no destination below the marking — not UNKNOWN, not CLASSIFIED, just blank, as if no one thought to fill it in. Director Walker uses the word "contributed" when describing RE-01's relationship to the Reset — not "caused," not "produced." He uses it three times. If asked about alternative names for the facility: "That's the reference code. We've standardized."

DIRECTOR CALLUM WALKER
DIVISION IV OPERATIONS OFFICER // COPENHAGEN HQ
SPIKE 0 — SERENE

What he is: A career operations officer who processes field assignments the way a machine processes inputs. He believes RE-01 is a recovery mission. He is not lying about this. He does not know there is anything else to believe.

WHAT HE WILL SAY

On Reeves: "One of four commissioned researchers. Presumed Reset like everyone else. We have no documentation of him post-Year 0."

On the lower floors: "The plans we have are the plans we have. Floor 2 shows a maintenance access point. Standard sub-basement infrastructure. Nothing flagged."

On finding personnel inside: "Handle with discretion." He does not elaborate.

"Bring back what's there. Leave with a clear structural assessment. That's the mission. Everything else is interpretation."
PETRA GARCIA
SCIENCE DIVISION TECHNICAL LIAISON
SPIKE 1 — STABLE

Early 30s. Takes notes on paper, not terminals. Watches the room more than she contributes to it. Present to answer technical questions about the official research mandate — she does so competently and does not expand beyond the question.

THE SLIP

Toward the end of the briefing, she sets a printed page in front of one party member and taps it twice with one finger. It lists:

REEVES, E. (US) // CALAFIORI, M. (Italy) // QI, L. (China) // GUMEDE, A. (South Africa)

If asked what it is: "The four researchers commissioned at the same time as RE-01. Background. In case it's useful." She doesn't say why it might be useful. She doesn't know why, exactly. Something about the mission sits at an angle she can't name.

02 // FORT WAYNE
SETTING // FORMER INDIANA, USA — EN ROUTE TO RE-01

Fort Wayne is the closest settlement to Diamond Hill — the local name for RE-01's site. It functions. Distribution center, community hall, working infrastructure. People living their lives with consistent, moderate efficiency.

Something is off about it. Not wrong, exactly. Just quieter than it should be, given how many people are present.

Two people at a café table are having a conversation. Their voices are normal. Nobody laughs. Not because anything sad is happening. Because nothing is funny enough to produce that particular response. A child stops at the memorial kiosk, reads three names, and continues walking — because that is what the child knows to do with such a list.

THE DISTRIBUTION CENTER
INTEL // EQUIPMENT

UGD-managed, clean, efficient. Pre-Reset brand labels on some containers — a cereal brand, a cleaning product. These feel uncanny.

■ IF MENTIONING DIAMOND HILL

The clerk's expression doesn't change: "Take enough water for four days. The facility is sealed. People who've gone in haven't always come out the same." Stated as a logistics note.

THE COMMUNITY HALL
INTEL // ATMOSPHERE

Social space. Conversations are pleasant, specific, and almost entirely practical. One man is telling a story. When he reaches the end, listeners agree it was a good story. Nobody is delighted. The correct response has occurred.

Tommy Garza tends bar. His information about Diamond Hill is the most useful the party will get before entering.

LENA VASEK
CIVILIAN RESIDENT // MEMORIAL SQUARE
SPIKE 3 — SHAKEN

Mid-40s. Standing near the memorial kiosk with the particular stillness of someone waiting for something they've forgotten they're waiting for. She doesn't know her readings are elevated. She checks her wand rarely.

"My brother's name is on the fourth panel. Matej. I know he was twelve years older than me. I know we lived in the same building until I was nine. I know that I was very close to him, according to the journals I kept before Year 0. I've read them several times."

A pause.

"They're quite detailed. I seem to have had very strong feelings about him. I can see that clearly in the writing. I can't find the feeling itself."

If anyone stays and listens: "I thought coming here would help. I'm not sure what 'help' would mean exactly. I've been coming here for two years. Something keeps bringing me back. I don't know what to call that."

She is not asking for anything. She will thank whoever spoke to her — with careful precision, not warmth — and turn back to the kiosk.

■ SPIKE TRIGGER // GM DISCRETION

A party member who engages with Lena honestly — not to gather information, but as a human being speaking to another human being — may make a SPIKE Save at TN 9. The trigger isn't the content. It's the gap: the distance between what she's describing and what she should be feeling, and what that says about the world they live in.

TOMMY GARZA
BARTENDER // COMMUNITY HALL
SPIKE 1 — STABLE

50s. Large. The kind of person who has stayed in one place while the world changed around him.

"Diamond Hill. Yeah. People went in, first couple years. Shelter-seekers, mostly — the facility was intact, had power, walls that held. Some came back. Some didn't. The ones that came back... they were elevated. Bad elevated. We stopped tracking them after a while."

On recent activity: "There was a group, three years ago. Five of them. One of them never came back. Brandon. Good man, before. He's still in there somewhere. Comes up to the fence sometimes. Won't come out." Tommy has been out there a few times, tried to call him. Brandon just stands there, then goes back inside.

He offers the party an extra round before they leave. He doesn't explain why.

03 // THE APPROACH
SETTING // RE-01 — RURAL MORGAN COUNTY, FORMER INDIANA

Forty minutes out of Fort Wayne, the road deteriorates — cracked asphalt, weeds splitting through, overhanging branches closing overhead. The facility appears gradually: first the fence line through the trees, then the bulk of the main structure above it.

The birdsong stops approximately 200 meters from the perimeter. There is no obvious reason for this.

THE PERIMETER FENCE

Chain-link, topped with wire. Electrification dead — cut or climb. Exception: turret posts at the southeast and southwest corners are still drawing power. Rubber/concussive rounds, non-lethal, but a direct hit suppresses and disorients. They track movement above the knee.

The fence has been repaired multiple times along the north side — new sections spliced into old, four different attempts visible, each cut from the inside.

NORTH ACCESS DOOR

A maintenance door set into the building's foundation, through a gap in the north fence that has been broken outward repeatedly. The door is unlocked. Opens onto a utility corridor connecting to Floor 1's administrative wing.

AWARENESS TN 9

The door frame has scratch marks at varying heights — concentrated around mid-chest, then lower, as though someone checked this door regularly for a long time. The most recent marks are closer to the ground.

04 // FLOOR 1 — UGD SANCTIONED RESEARCH
■ FLOOR 1 // THE PUBLIC FACE

Emergency lighting — half operational. Air sealed-facility stale: the absence of weather, seasons, anything variable. The building is intact. Floor 1 should feel, initially, like what the briefing described. The wrongness is in the details, not the structure.

RECEPTION & ADMINISTRATION
■ FIND

Sign-in log at reception. Last entry: TAMSIN VORKE, CONTRACT TECHNICIAN. Sign-in time: 6:14 AM. No sign-out.

■ FIND — SUPPLY ROOM

Cold storage containers labeled PS-02 and PS-06 without supporting documentation. Empty and clean.

■ FIND — ADMINISTRATION OFFICES

Personnel transfer notices signed only with Reeves' initials: E.R. At regular intervals. No destination listed. One file cabinet tab (missed on first pass, or revealed after Floor 5's emotional weight): "IDEOLOGICAL REVIEW — PENDING." Cabinet is empty. Handwritten note on the tab: "Unsuitable for continuation. Removed."

Delivery manifests filed under HVAC MAINTENANCE from supplier AP Logistics — eleven shipments across 14 months, labeled as HVAC components. The weights and dimensions don't match any HVAC equipment in the building.

■ FIND — AP LOGISTICS (IF INVESTIGATED)

One manifest has a handwritten notation: "AP Logistics contact: Alan Prater, Regional Director." No other details. The name means nothing to the players now.

COMMUNICATIONS ROOM

Radio equipment. Active jamming — still running on emergency power.

TECH TN 11

The system wasn't designed to prevent all communication. It was designed to prevent outbound communication to UGD infrastructure specifically. Internal frequencies work. A technician's log: final entry reads "Jamming configuration locked per Dr. R. directive. Frequency exclusion confirmed. Tested and functional." No further entries.

THE CAFETERIA

Tables still set. Meals abandoned mid-morning. Whatever happened, it wasn't at mealtime — people left during the working day. Some trays overturned. Most haven't.

■ FIND — PERSONAL NOTEBOOK (SIGNED K.)
"Something happened in the lower wing last night. Vorke won't say what. R. said equipment failure but they ran the fans all night and the air smelled wrong. I'm going to ask him directly tomorrow."

There is no entry for the following day.

THE STAIRWELL ACCESS

Stairs descend to Floor 2. A second stairwell behind a keypad-locked door descends further. This door does not appear on the briefing floor plans.

INVESTIGATION TN 9

The door to the lower stairwell has been struck repeatedly from below — dents in the metal at inconsistent heights. The most recent impacts are low.

05 // FLOOR 2 — RESTRICTED EXPANSION
■ FLOOR 2 // THE OFFICIAL RESEARCH, AND SOMETHING ELSE BEGINNING

Floor 2 has more power than Floor 1 — the generator prioritizes lab equipment. Fluorescent overhead lighting, most of it still working. The spaces are larger. The research is the same as Floor 1, until it isn't. The atmosphere shifts mid-floor: labs transition from clinical to technical-industrial. Documentation begins using terminology that doesn't appear in any briefing material.

LABORATORY SUITES

Standard neurological research equipment. EEG rigs. A gene sequencing lab with consumables still racked. The research documented here is legitimate — the aerosol therapy trials are real, the EEG baseline work is real. This is the version of RE-01 the UGD paid for.

■ FIND — LAB NOTEBOOKS

References to PS-series assessments that are not explained. Entries note "signal receptivity" as a measurement alongside standard neurological baselines. These terms appear without definition, like jargon whose meaning is assumed.

ANIMAL WING

Cages, cleaned. Medical waste containers, sealed. The animal trials ended well before the facility went dark — the wing was shut down and sanitized. What replaced the animal trials is not documented here.

■ FIND — RESEARCH SUMMARY (PINNED)

A laminated summary pinned to the wall describes outcomes from "aerosol delivery trials" in terms of "integration efficacy" and "semantic continuity preservation." The language is technical and assumes shared knowledge the reader doesn't have. No definition of what was being integrated, or why continuity needed preserving.

SERVER ROOM & FREIGHT ELEVATOR

The server room is locked — TECH TN 13 to access, or accessible via a keycard found on Floor 3. Data terminals within show a directory with folder structures labeled by date and PS designation. The files themselves require deeper access.

The freight elevator marked MAINTENANCE ACCESS on the briefing maps operates. Its panel shows five buttons: 1, 2, and three unmarked positions below. The elevator descends.

06 // FLOORS 3–4 — THE DESCENT
■ FLOOR 3 // WHERE THE RESEARCH CHANGES

Floor 3 is not on the briefing documents. The air is different — circulated differently, processed. The aesthetic shifts: fluorescent lighting replaced by task lighting in specific zones only. The walls are lined with equipment that has no analog on the floors above. Something industrial happened here.

RESEARCH ARCHIVE
■ FIND — CALAFIORI CORRESPONDENCE

Letters referencing the Italian facility in Genoa. The tone escalates from collegial to heated. Calafiori's method is described as "incendiary correction" — he disagrees with Reeves' approach. Reeves' responses are measured, cold, and do not engage with the ethical objections.

A burned notice, tucked into the back of a drawer: "The Calafiori facility — Genoa, Italy — has gone silent. Fire reported at the facility approximately six weeks ago. Total loss of structure and personnel. No survivors recovered."

The fire occurred before RE-01 went dark. Reeves knew his Italian counterpart was gone. He kept working anyway.

■ FIND — GUMEDE NANITE MEMO
"Nanite shipment received from Dr. Gumede (South African facility). Count: 4,800 units. Storage temperature verified. Awaiting integration protocol. Preliminary analysis confirms Gumede's design specifications — these are significantly smaller than our prototype batch. Recalibration application potential: unknown. Further consultation with Gumede's team recommended before deployment."

The players do not know what nanites are or why they matter. They have a name: Gumede. One of the four on Garcia's list.

■ FLOOR 4 // BRANDON COLE

Floor 4 is maintenance infrastructure — pipes, conduit, mechanical systems for the floors above and below. Emergency lighting only. Some sections are in complete darkness. The air recycler runs louder here. And somewhere in the walls or ceilings of this floor, something moves.

BRANDON COLE
CIVILIAN // TRAPPED IN RE-01 — THREE YEARS
SPIKE 11 — CONSUMED

He is not an NPC to interview. He is a person in the final stages of neurological collapse. When the party locates him — in the animal wing, on Floor 4, or if he finds them — he recognizes human presence and destabilizes immediately. He backs against the wall. Climbs. Tries to get above eye level. Makes sounds before language arrives.

"THE DRONES WENT UP — I SAW —"

"DON'T LET THEM — DON'T LET THEM —"

"THE TANKS WERE EMPTY — THEY WERE EMPTY AND THEN THEY WEREN'T —"

"IT'S STILL RUNNING. IT'S STILL RUNNING DOWN THERE. I CAN HEAR IT."
■ SPIKE TRIGGER — PROXIMITY TN 11 (DISADVANTAGE IF SPIKE 5+)

Any party member at SPIKE 5+ within 10 meters of Brandon must make a SPIKE Save at the start of their turn. This is emotional contagion — proximity to someone at CONSUMED level is dangerous.

GROUNDING BRANDON

Possible but extremely difficult. d20 + MIND vs TN 14, at Disadvantage. One attempt per operative. Success does not restore him — it produces approximately 90 seconds of fractured lucidity. In that window he can answer questions, though not in linear sequence. He will not come out of the building. He does not believe he can survive outside it anymore. He may not be wrong.

If grounded: he looks at whoever did it for a long moment. Then: "You shouldn't have come down here." Not as a threat. As a medical assessment.

WHAT HE KNOWS (LUCID)

He entered RE-01 three years ago with four others looking for shelter. The other four left within a week. Brandon stayed — something in the building kept pulling him back. He has been in the sub-levels for most of the last two years. He knows the facility the way animals know territory: by feel, by sound, by pattern.

  • The tanks on Floor 5 were emptied "all at once, a long time ago" — drones carried whatever was in them upward and outward. He watched from the vents.
  • Something is still running on Floor 5. Not mechanical. He can't describe it better than that.
  • He has been in PS-08. He stopped going after the first few times. "There's someone in there. Or there was."
  • On Alan Prater: the name makes him flinch. He's seen it on crates. "Those weren't HVAC parts."
07 // FLOOR 5 — THE REAL FACILITY
■ FLOOR 5 // WHERE THE RESEARCH ALWAYS ENDED
■ GM NOTE — HOW TO RUN FLOOR 5

Floor 5 should feel like stepping into the inside of something's mind. The scale is wrong for a sub-basement. The ceiling is higher than it should be. The space opens in ways that contradict the building's footprint above. The emergency lighting here is red — a design decision, not a fault.

Play it slowly. Let the party move through the space before putting language to anything. The discoveries should arrive in silence first.

THE PRODUCTION FLOOR
PRIMARY FIND

Enormous tank arrays — twelve in total, each the size of a shipping container. All empty. Drain manifolds still connected to industrial dispersal systems that route upward through the building and outward to the roof. The tanks have been cleaned thoroughly. No residue.

Stenciled on each tank: BACTERIAL SUSPENSION — NEUROLOGICAL APPLICATION SERIES — DR. E. REEVES IV

■ FIND — DISPERSAL LOG

A single laminated sheet bolted to the wall above the primary valve manifold. A dispersal record. Twelve tanks. One event. Date: Year 0, Day 1. Duration: 6 hours, 14 minutes. Distribution method: HIGH-ALTITUDE DRONE RELAY. Coverage zone: GLOBAL. Outcome field is blank.

■ SPIKE TRIGGER — COMPREHENSION TN 13

Any operative who reads the dispersal log in full and makes a successful Investigation check to understand what it describes must make a SPIKE Save at TN 13. Not because the document is disturbing in its language — it isn't. Because of what the language means, once assembled. The Reset was delivered from this room.

REEVES' LABORATORY
PRIMARY FIND

A private workspace set apart from the production floor by a glass partition — floor to ceiling, clean, intact. Reeves' desk is ordered. His work is filed. The man was methodical to the end.

■ FIND — PRIMARY RESEARCH JOURNALS

Twelve volumes. The early volumes describe legitimate research. The later volumes describe the Reset — not by that name. He calls it THE RECALIBRATION. The language is technical and proud. The emotional register across all twelve volumes is consistent: measured, certain, and entirely without doubt.

Volume 11 contains a single sentence, underlined twice, that doesn't match the register of anything around it:

"The Prime Signal must originate from a living source. Synthetic generation is possible for short durations but experiences rapid entropy. Sustained global application requires a sustained biological anchor."

This is all the players need. They will not understand what a Prime Signal is. They should not. The word anchor should land.

■ FIND — THE ITALY FILE

A single manila folder labeled CALAFIORI — GENOA. Inside: two photographs of the Italian facility, pre-fire. An address. A handwritten note: "Incendiary approach — theoretically sound, practically irreversible. His conclusions re: emotional cauterization are consistent with my own re: baseline dampening. Different vectors. Same destination."

The address is Genoa, Italy. The fire destroyed the building. The research — and whoever survived — may still be findable.

■ FIND — PRATER CORRESPONDENCE (PARTIAL)

Three letters signed A.P. Tone is businesslike, then increasingly pressured. The final letter: "The timeline cannot slip further. The WHO sessions are not going to produce anything we can use. If you want this to happen, it happens on our schedule." Reeves' draft response, handwritten in the margin: "Agreed. Initiating."

THE PS WING
PRIMARY FIND

A corridor of eight chambers. Numbered PS-01 through PS-08. Each chamber is approximately four meters square, sealed with heavy glass doors. Observation windows. Interior monitoring equipment, still drawing minimal power. Each chamber has a subject nameplate. PS-01 through PS-07 are empty — cleaned, sealed, unremarkable except for the scale of their containment infrastructure, which is far beyond what any aerosol therapy trial would require.

PS-08 is not empty.

■ PS-08

The nameplate reads: MITCHELL, C. — PS-08 — PRIME CANDIDATE.

The glass door is broken from the inside — shattered outward into the corridor. The chamber beyond is intact: bed, monitoring equipment, a small desk. On the desk: a series of etchings scratched into the surface with what appears to have been a pen nib. Hundreds of them. Geometric patterns that don't resolve into anything recognizable. Along the base of the wall: more etchings, lower and lower as they progress around the room, ending near the floor in the far corner.

A single piece of paper on the floor near the broken glass:

"Even at the end of the world, humanity cannot resist a flair for the dramatic."

No signature. No date. Whether Mitchell wrote it, or Reeves, or someone else entirely, the document does not say. The players should not know.

■ SPIKE TRIGGER — PS-08 ENTRY TN 11

Entering the chamber: optional save for any operative. The trigger is not a body — there is no body. It is the etchings. The sheer volume of them. The implication of the time they required, and what state of mind produced them.

INVESTIGATION TN 9

The etchings near the floor are different from the rest — not geometric. They are letters, scratched smaller and smaller as they approach the corner. They read: PRATER — HARDWARE — THE DRONES. Then a single word, repeated: STILL. STILL. STILL.

08 // EXTRACTION & DEBRIEF
LEAVING RE-01

The party exits the way they came. The turrets track them through the yard. The birdsong resumes the moment they clear the fence line. The silence of the facility is immediately behind them and immediately absent.

The drive back to Fort Wayne is however long the players need it to be. If anyone says something, let them. If no one says anything, let that stand too. Both are correct responses to Floor 5.

TOMMY GARZA — IF THEY RETURN

Tommy doesn't ask what they found. He asks about Brandon. Whatever the party tells him, his response is the same: he processes it and then pours them a drink without asking if they want one. "He was a good man before." He said that already. He says it again. It means something different now.

THE DEBRIEF — COPENHAGEN

Director Walker receives the report professionally. He thanks the party. He schedules a documentation review. He does not ask follow-up questions about the lower floors until prompted, and when prompted: "We'll need to flag that for a structural assessment team." He means it. He has no idea what he's flagging.

The journals, the dispersal log, the Calafiori file — all submitted. Walker reviews none of it in front of the party. It will go to Science Division. Garcia may receive it. That is a later session's thread.

■ GM NOTE — THE DEBRIEF TONE

The debrief should feel anticlimactic by design. The party has just seen the inside of the facility where the world was changed. They are now sitting in a conference room describing it to a man who is filing it. The institutional indifference is the horror.

09 // MILESTONE — THE FLIGHT HOME
■ MILESTONE TRIGGER // THE RETURN FLIGHT TO COPENHAGEN

The milestone occurs on the flight home — not after the debrief, not after rest, but in the air between Indiana and Denmark. The liminal space is intentional. The party is suspended between what they found and what comes next. They have time to think. They have no duties. They are not yet home.

Award the milestone when at least one of the following is true: a party member has articulated, in-character, what the dispersal log means. Or the conversation has turned to Italy. Or someone has said something suggesting they understand, even partially, that the world they serve may not be what they were told.

Each operative chooses one:

  • Gain a new Skill (Trained, +2)
  • Upgrade a Trained Skill to Specialized (+3)
  • Gain a Trait (see Player's Guide — max 6)
  • Increase max COMPOSURE by +1
■ GM NOTE — HOW TO GIVE THE MILESTONE

Step outside the fiction for a moment. Tell them they've reached a milestone. Let them choose. Then return to the flight. Whatever conversation was happening — continue it. The milestone is a beat, not an ending. The flight lands. Campaign 2 is Italy. They don't know that yet.

THE HOOK FORWARD

The Calafiori file contains an address in Genoa. The fire destroyed the building three years before RE-01 went dark. Calafiori's research — incendiary therapy, "emotional cauterization" — was consistent with Reeves' approach by Reeves' own assessment. Something survived that fire, or someone did. Reeves knew it, and kept working anyway.

The players may pursue this themselves. If not, Garcia will make contact between sessions — a short message, nothing more: "The Genoa address. I looked it up. The building was rebuilt. Different name now. You might want to go."
